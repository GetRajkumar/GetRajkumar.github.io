ReactModule FederationRspackMicro-Frontends

Re-architecting an Angular monolith into 11 React micro-frontends

Frontend Technical Lead · EY · Team of 5 · 2022–present

A multi-tenant, enterprise-scale platform had outgrown its single Angular codebase — teams blocked each other and builds were slow. I led the re-architecture into 11 independently-deployable React micro-frontends (separate repos) wired with Module Federation, behind a shell container owning routing, auth, and shared dependencies.

  • Migrated the build from react-scripts to Rspack — production builds ~2 min → ~5 sec (≈24×).
  • Releases moved from every 2 months to biweekly, with independent team delivery.
  • Engineered smooth rendering of a ~40MB dataset in a single view, shared across micro-frontends.

Frontend Technical Lead · EY

Frontend Technical Lead · EY
